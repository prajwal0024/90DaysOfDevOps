# Plan for 90DaysOfDevOps

## Current Level

Working professional with 5+ years of experience in web development, primarily working with React, JavaScript, Node.js, and GraphQL. I am starting #90DaysOfDevOps to strengthen my ability to build, deploy, operate, and troubleshoot production applications.

## 3 Goals for 90DaysOfDevOps

1. Gain exposure to production-grade DevOps workflows.
2. Learn to monitor and manage production applications.
3. Consistently document and share my learning journey in public.

## 3 Core DevOps Skills I Would Like to Build

1. **Docker and Kubernetes:** Understand containerization and the basics of container orchestration.
2. **CI/CD Automation:** Learn to automate application testing, builds, and deployments.
3. **Cloud and Monitoring:** Understand cloud fundamentals and learn to monitor and troubleshoot applications.

## Weekly Commitment

- **Weekdays:** 1–2 hours per day.
- **Weekends:** 2–3 hours per day.
